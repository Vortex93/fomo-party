03Play a little of everything. Ready up, answer, draw, vote, and remember. Scores carry across the playlist.

All five games need at least four players together. With two or three players, the host can choose a smaller playlist. The TV view is optional and never counts as a player.

Be kind. The host can pause or skip a round at any time. Secret roles are private to each phone. Keep memory screens hidden after the reveal—this is a friendly game, not a cheat-proof competition.
